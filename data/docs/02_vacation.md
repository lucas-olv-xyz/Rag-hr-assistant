# 1. Vacation entitlement

## 1.1 Acquisition period
After 12 months of employment, the employee earns the right to vacation. This period is called the acquisition period.

## 1.2 Duration
Each acquisition period gives the right to **30 consecutive days** of vacation, paid with an additional one-third of the salary.

## 1.3 Reduction for unjustified absences
The number of vacation days decreases with unjustified absences during the acquisition period:

| Unjustified absences | Vacation days |
|---|---|
| up to 5 | 30 days |
| 6 to 14 | 24 days |
| 15 to 23 | 18 days |
| 24 to 32 | 12 days |
| more than 32 | the employee loses the right to vacation for that period |

# 2. Splitting
Vacation can be split into up to 3 periods. One of them must have at least 14 days, and each of the others at least 5 days.

# 3. Request
Vacation requests are submitted in the HR system at least 30 days before the start date. Approval depends on the team's schedule.

# 4. Cash-out of vacation
The employee may convert up to one-third of the vacation (10 days) into cash. The request must be made up to 15 days before the end of the acquisition period.
