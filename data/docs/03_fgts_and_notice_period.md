# 1. FGTS

## 1.1 Monthly deposit
The company deposits **8% of the gross salary** every month into the employee's FGTS account. FGTS is Brazil's mandatory severance guarantee fund.

## 1.2 Checking the balance
The FGTS balance can be checked in the Caixa Econômica Federal app, using the employee's PIS or NIS number.

# 2. Notice period

## 2.1 Dismissal without cause (initiated by the company)
- 30 days of notice.
- Plus 3 days for each full year of service.
- Total limit of 90 days.
- *Example:* 2 full years of service gives 36 days of notice.

## 2.2 Resignation (initiated by the employee)
- The employee must work **30 days** of notice.
- There are no extra days for length of service in this case.

## 2.3 Waiving the notice period
If both parties agree, the notice period may be shortened. The agreement must be in writing.
