# 1. Salary bands

## 1.1 Junior
Band of R$ 5,500 to R$ 7,000 per month.

## 1.2 Mid-level
Band of R$ 7,000 to R$ 10,000 per month.

## 1.3 Senior
Band of R$ 10,000 to R$ 14,000 per month.

## 1.4 Review
The bands are reviewed every year, in March, and approved by the HR director.

*This is a fictional internal policy. Access is restricted to the HR team.*
