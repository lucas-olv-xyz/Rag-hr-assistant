# 1. Working hours

## 1.1 Duration
The standard schedule at DataFlow Brasil is 8 hours per day and 44 hours per week, Monday to Friday, with a lunch break of at least 1 hour.

## 1.2 Time tracking
Every employee clocks in and out in the electronic time-tracking system. Recording is mandatory and is the basis for overtime calculations.

# 2. Overtime

## 2.1 Definition
Overtime is time worked beyond the standard 8 hours per day. The limit is 2 overtime hours per day, so a maximum of 10 hours worked in one day.

## 2.2 Premium
- **Working days:** a 50% premium over the regular hourly rate.
- **Sundays and public holidays:** a 100% premium over the regular hourly rate.
- Compensatory time off replaces payment only if it is agreed in writing with the employee.

## 2.3 Calculation example
An employee with a monthly salary of R$ 6,000.00 (220 hours per month):
- Regular hourly rate: R$ 27.27
- Overtime on a working day (50%): R$ 40.91
- Overtime on a Sunday or holiday (100%): R$ 54.55

## 2.4 Working on Sundays
Sunday work happens only when authorized by management and follows the published schedule. The 100% premium in section 2.2 applies.

# 3. Daily limit
The total limit is 10 hours per day, counting regular hours and overtime. Work beyond that limit happens only in exceptional cases, with immediate notice to HR.
